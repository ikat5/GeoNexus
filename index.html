How it works

From one click to two rovers on the move.

01

Pick a site

Mars or the Moon turns slowly on its axis. Click any spot and it stops, pinned where you chose.

02

Meet its Earth twin

The terrain model takes your site’s features and hands back the closest match on Earth, with a side-by-side comparison.

03

Drive both

A rover sets off at each site. Gravity, slope and thin or thick air change how it moves.

Readouts

Read the ground like a mission team.

Every site gets the same five readings, so a crater floor on Mars and a high desert on Earth can be compared at a glance.

Speed
How fast the rover rolls under local gravity.
Elevation
Height of the site above the planet’s reference surface.
Slope
How steep the ground is under the wheels.
Air density
Thin Martian air, no air on the Moon, thick air at home.
Temperature
Surface temperature in kelvin, measured and inferred by the rover.
